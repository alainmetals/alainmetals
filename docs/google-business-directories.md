## Google Business Profile

**Category:** Precious Metal Dealer
**Secondary Categories:** Jewelry Store, Gemstone Dealer

**Description:**
AL AIN Metals Corp is a private client precious metals house in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania. We provide direct access to independently assayed physical gold (99.5%-99.99% purity) and GIA/GRS-certified gemstones for UHNWIs, family offices, and private bank desks. All gold is assayed by SGS or Bureau Veritas, with full provenance documentation and Lloyd's-of-London insurance on all shipments via Brink's or Loomis. Minimum allocation: USD $250,000. Trial allocations from $50,000 for verified family offices. No brokers — direct principals only.

**Service Area:** Worldwide (serving international private clients)
**Areas Served:** Dar es Salaam, Arusha, Nairobi, Dubai, Singapore, Geneva, London, New York

**Services:**

- GIA/GRS gemstone certification
- Lloyd's-of-London insured shipping
- Brink's/Loomis secure logistics
- Vaulting services (Dubai DMCC, Singapore Brink's, Geneva Loomis)
- Provenance documentation
- Chain of custody verification
